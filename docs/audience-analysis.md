# Audience analysis — Libya Global Reports

**Evidence base:** Product copy and structure in `apps/libya-global-reports` (board, methodology, source register, sample article patterns). **No interviews were conducted.** Anything about real organisations is labelled unknown.

**Decision context:** After year 1, organisations may pay for depth. The public board stays free. Ads and a full-site paywall are out of charter.

---

### Jobs-to-be-done (compressed)

The board’s own lede is the reader job: what changed, where it matters, operational implications, what to watch next. Sample articles consistently separate **attributed statements** from **corroborated facts**, and **missing** from **normal**.

The buyer job (inferred from that design, not from interviews) is: **keep a desk aligned on those four questions without each person maintaining a private clip file.** Depth that might be worth paying for, once trust exists: inbox delivery, 90-day memory, sector filter as a pack, seats, alerts on *published* items. Depth that this product will not sell: probabilities, live risk gauges, a nationwide parallel FX, or exclusive paywalled facts that disappear from the public board.

### What “depth” must contain before an organisation would pay

From the methodology and sample patterns — not from named buyers:

1. **Non-demo cadence.** SAMPLE on the header is an explicit reason not to pay.
2. **Conflict visible.** Ras Lanuf-style dual attribution; no averaged “market view”.
3. **Official vs parallel FX rules** already specified; missing/stale/incompatible quotes refused.
4. **Infrastructure humility.** Not a GECOL/LTT comprehensive monitor.
5. **Watch-next that is operational**, not rhetorical.
6. **Corrections that stay on the page.**
7. **Public board still free** so the org is paying for *habit and memory*, not for hiding facts.

Unknown without conversations: which of email, archive, sector pack, seats, or alerts they would pay for first; what language mix they need; whether same-day public + paid “faster email” is acceptable.

### Paywall objection (charter)

A full-site paywall conflicts with humanitarian and public readers in the same sector mix. The chosen model (free board, paid depth) is the response. Ads would read as the opposite of “no tracking cookies” and the methodology’s professional register.

### Unknowns (do not fill)

- Named organisations, headcount, or budget holders.
- Whether desks already buy a competitor wire.
- Price.
- Whether Arabic must exist before any paid SKU.
